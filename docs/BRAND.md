# Brand

The reference for all copy and positioning. Read before writing any user-facing text.

## Positioning (one line)
An AI engineer who *ships* agentic systems — proof-first, not adjective-first.

## The tension to resolve
Early-career, bold positioning. The fix is NOT to dial back the claim — it's to let the WORK
carry it. Every bold statement sits next to a working artifact. The site itself proves frontend
+ taste in 3 seconds; the case studies prove AI + architecture. Never assert seniority; show it.

## Voice
Smart, dry, occasionally weird. Confident without trying hard.
- NO: "passionate about AI", "cutting-edge solutions", "leveraging synergies", LinkedIn-speak,
  hustle-bro energy.
- YES: concrete and a little pointed. e.g. "I build agents that take actions, not chatbots that
  answer questions."

## Audience (one spine serves both)
- Hiring: CTOs, eng leaders, founders.
- Clients: founders, design-literate teams wanting AI built right.
- Same proof serves both. Optional single fork near the CTA: "hiring" vs "got a project" ->
  same contact form, different framing. Do NOT build two sites.

## Proof assets (the spine of "selected work")
- Agentic e-commerce platform (architecture flex; multi-agent, real actions).
- Resume Analyzer — live on Hugging Face Spaces (a working deployed AI app > any cert).
- MD ministry AI platform (FastAPI, RAG, schema-dynamic) — production work.

## Visual direction
Dark, cinematic, GPU-driven. One signature 3D moment that MEANS something (an agent
reasoning/acting loop), not decorative geometry. Restrained confident type. One accent color.
The taste signals judgment — worth more than seniority claims for an early-career engineer.
